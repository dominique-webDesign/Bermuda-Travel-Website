Why Visit Bermuda?

Imagine taking a cruise or even stepping off of a plane and the first thing you feel is the warmness all around you with the beautiful view of the pink sand right before your eyes.

And what's even better is that Bermuda is a small island. Meaning that it's easier to access more activities on the island

Horseshoe Bay Beach

Fun Fact: The sand gets its pink color from the tiny red organisms on the reefs that are mixed with coral, and crushed shells. The waves then wash the pink colored sand onto the shore.

Best Beaches To Visit

  • Horseshoe Bay
  • Elbow Beach
  • Warwick Long Bay
  • Astwood Park

Activities to Do

Bermuda has a lot to offer, including historic streets, places to eat, and beautiful scenery.

Top 5 Activities

  • Diving or snorkeling at the coral reefs
  • Climbing the Gibbs Hill Lighthouse
  • Exploring the Crystal Caves
  • Riding the Ferry around the Island
  • Walking St. George's Streets
