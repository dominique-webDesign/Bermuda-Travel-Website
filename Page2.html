Food and Culture

Bermuda isn't only known for its beaches, it's actually known for its pastel houses and buildings all around.

The food in Bermuda is also a pretty well known highlight as well.

A Wonderful Time For Food

  1. Fish Chowder (Breakfast)
  2. Bermuda Fish Sandwich (Lunch)
  3. Rum Swizzle (Dinner)
  4. Rum Cake (Dessert)

What to Pack

  • Suncreen and a bathing suit
  • Light Jacket for when it's cold
  • A Ferry and Bus pass
  • Bring everything that is required for the trip
